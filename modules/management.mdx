The Management Module is the control center of your Flowy workspace. Every repository you have scanned lives here, presented as a structured panel of interactive maps. Rather than hunting through browser tabs or scattered bookmarks, you get a single hub where you can locate any project at a glance, check whether your map reflects the latest commit on your branch, and organize everything into a structure that mirrors how you actually think about your work.

## Organizing your maps

Folders and tags work together to give you a flexible filing system for every project you track in Flowy. Folders represent broad groupings — Personal Projects, Open Source, Work — while tags let you label maps with any additional context you need.

You can move any map into a folder at any time, and apply as many tags as are useful. The dashboard updates immediately, so your workspace always reflects how you currently think about your repositories.

<Tip>
  Use tags to separate work and personal repositories even when they live in the same folder — for example, tagging freelance client projects separately from personal side projects so you can filter quickly during a session.
</Tip>

## Global Search

Global Search lets you find a specific file or function across all your saved maps instantly. Instead of opening each map in turn, you query the entire workspace from one place and navigate directly to the result.

Search works across every map you have saved in Flowy. Results reflect the state of your maps at the time they were last synced — if a repository has new commits that have not yet been rescanned, the newest files may not appear until the map is updated.

## Understanding the Git Sync indicator

Each map in the dashboard displays a traffic light indicator alongside the branch name. The indicator tells you at a glance whether the map is current with the branch's last commit or whether new commits have landed since the last scan.

Click the indicator to trigger a manual rescan, or configure Auto-Push Webhooks in the [Integrations module](/modules/integrations) to keep maps up to date automatically whenever a merge lands.
